9ρ next second, day of week × hour

Each cell is the median over every hour at that JST weekday and clock hour. Pooling all weeks averages out most of the week-to-week swings in section 8, so what remains is the shape of the trading week. Days are JST calendar days, so Saturday 0:00–6:00 JST is still Friday in New York.

10Lead volatility, day of week × hour

Median realised volatility of the lead market's 100ms returns in that hour, bps per hour. Set against section 9: where both are dark, the delayed response is strong because the market is busy.

11Lag spread, day of week × hour

Median spread of the lag market at the report's execution size in that hour, bps; darker is wider. Market makers quote wider when the market moves, so within the week this mostly follows section 10. The part that does not, such as a wide spread in a quiet hour, points to a thinner book.
